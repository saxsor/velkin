Frequently asked questions

Where do I start if I don't know what my business needs?

Start with the Velkin Audit. In 5–7 business days you'll have a full diagnosis that tells you exactly what to move first. The MX$3,500 cost is credited if you later hire any package.

What's the difference between Velkin Lite and Velkin Start?

Lite (from MX$14,000) is a single-page landing with a basic logo and a form connected directly to WhatsApp — built for businesses that need to be online now, without the full commitment of a larger site. Start (from MX$28,000) includes a complete logo system, a 3 to 5 page website, and a CRM with automatic notifications. If Lite isn't enough, the natural move is to scale up to Start.

Do prices include VAT?

Published prices do not include VAT (IVA). The 16% applies to the total under current Mexican tax law.

Can I hire Velkin Grow without going through Start or Scale first?

Yes, as long as you already have active Instagram and Facebook accounts. Grow doesn't require having worked with Velkin before. If your social presence is starting from zero, it's worth starting with Start first.

What's the difference between Velkin Pulse, Spark, Ops, and Grow?

All four run on the same engine, Velkin Content Hub, split across two axes: who operates it (you or Velkin) and whether it includes AI. Pulse (from MX$3,500/mo) is for businesses that want to schedule and approve their own content, backed by real metrics, no AI. Spark (from MX$7,500/mo) is the same as Pulse but with AI-generated content strategy and image generation. Ops (from MX$5,500/mo) is like Pulse but operated by Velkin — we coordinate the calendar and quality, no AI. Grow (from MX$12,000/mo) is the complete tier: Velkin runs everything, with AI-generated content strategy, a biweekly results report, and Meta Ads campaigns.

Do you work with companies outside of Mexico?

Yes. We work with clients across Mexico, LATAM, and Europe. We have team members in Mexico and Germany, giving us coverage across multiple time zones and direct knowledge of both markets.

How many revision rounds does each project include?

Revision rounds are specified in each project's proposal. We work with partial deliveries and stage-by-stage approvals to make sure the result is aligned before moving forward.

How long does Velkin Grow take to set up?

The flow is configured and tested in 5–7 business days: Content Hub, Meta Ads campaigns, and the reporting flow. Before delivery, we run live tests with real content. After that, the monthly operation starts and is billed month by month.
