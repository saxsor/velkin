The starting point

Photos ready, but publishing was still manual work.

La Lente de Pipus didn't need a website to get started. She needed a lightweight way to turn her photos into consistent social content, without going through the repetitive cycle of editing, deciding format, writing captions, and publishing by hand.

Velkin Content Hub — our own scheduling and publishing system — works here as an assistant: it receives the photo, asks whether she wants filters, asks whether it'll be a post or a story, generates captions with AI, lets her add more context if the tone isn't quite right, and only publishes once everything is approved.

Velkin Content Hub, with creative control

It's not a bot publishing blind. The flow asks, proposes, adjusts with context, and waits for approval before sending to Instagram or Facebook.
